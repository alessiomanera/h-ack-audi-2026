# Prompt Canva AI per il template della presentazione

Incollare in Canva AI (crea presentazione). Se Canva taglia il testo, incollare fino a "Create these slides:" e chiedere l'elenco slide come seconda richiesta. Dopo la generazione: salvare i colori hex come colori del brand e impostare Archivo e Inter nel Brand Kit (se disponibile), poi condividere con il team in modifica.

```text
Create a 16:9 presentation template for a hackathon pitch to Audi Italia's marketing director, a three-time Le Mans winner and Italian media and innovation leaders. Visual world: the Audi Revolut F1 Team and Audi's "clarity" design philosophy: precise, technical, calm, premium understatement. Avoid: gradients, neon, glow, emoji, cartoon illustrations, generic stock photos, busy collages.

Colors, use exactly: page background Carbon #161616; cards and panels Graphite #2B2929; main text Chalk #F5F2F2; secondary text Titanium #B3AFAF; dividers #423F3F. One accent only: Lava Red #FF2D00, at most one red element per slide (a thin rule, one key number, or a progress mark). Two optional light slides: background Chalk #F5F2F2 with Graphite #2B2929 text.

Typography: headings Archivo Bold, large, left-aligned; small uppercase labels with wide letter spacing; body Inter Regular. Max two text sizes per slide plus the label.

Layout: 12-column grid, wide margins, a lot of negative space, one message per slide, thin 1px lines, slide number and a small source line bottom-left in Titanium. Photo areas are full-bleed, dark, studio-lit cars or motorsport details.

All placeholder text in Italian. Create these slides:
1. Cover: project name, one-line claim, team name, "H-ACK · Co-Design the Future · 10.10.2026", large dark car photo area.
2. Il problema: one sentence and one big number with its source.
3. La voce dei giovani: three short peer quotes and "Abbiamo chiesto a N coetanei".
4. L'insight: a single bold statement with a thin Lava Red rule.
5. L'idea: name, one sentence, phone mockup on the right.
6. Come funziona: four-step horizontal journey with thin line icons.
7. Demo: two phone mockups side by side (vista utente, vista concessionario) and a QR code placeholder.
8. Gli asset Audi: four photo tiles (Formula 1, Le Mans, quattro e Dolomiti, e-tron).
9. Fattibilità: simple table of costs, timeline and who does what.
10. KPI a 6 mesi: three stat tiles with big numbers.
11. Perché Audi, perché ora: three short points.
12. Grazie: team names and roles, QR code to the live demo.
Also add one section-divider layout and one "Fonti" appendix slide.
```
